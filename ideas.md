# Design Brainstorm: Top 20 Tax Strategy Social Media Posts 2026

## Chosen Approach: Editorial Research Dashboard

**Design Movement:** Modern editorial / data-journalism aesthetic
**Core Principles:**
1. Clean, structured layout that separates Facebook and Instagram results into distinct visual zones
2. #00aa91 as the primary brand color, used for accents, headers, and interactive elements
3. Card-based post presentation with clear hierarchy of information
4. Professional, research-report tone with subtle depth and texture

**Color Philosophy:** #00aa91 (teal-green) as the signature color, paired with a clean white background and dark slate text. The teal-green conveys financial growth, trust, and professionalism. Secondary colors include light teal tints for section backgrounds and muted grays for metadata.

**Layout Paradigm:** Two-column split for Facebook vs Instagram sections, with a shared header and filterable card grid. Each post is a self-contained card with structured fields.

**Typography System:** 
- Headers: Bold, condensed sans-serif (e.g., "Space Grotesk" or "Sora")
- Body: Clean readable sans-serif (e.g., "Inter" or "DM Sans")
- Metadata: Monospace for dates and metrics

**Signature Elements:**
- Platform badges (Facebook blue vs Instagram gradient) for quick visual sorting
- Engagement metric pills with icon accents
- "Why it worked" callout boxes with subtle teal background

**Interaction Philosophy:** Smooth scroll, hover-to-expand cards, filter tabs to switch between platforms

**Brand Essence:** A professional research dashboard for tax strategy social media intelligence.

## Style Decisions

- Results are organized as distinct platform analytical chapters (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn, X), never as one undifferentiated feed.
- Every post card exposes four first-glance signals: rank badge, platform badge, engagement metric pills, and a teal "why it worked" insight preview.
- The page reads as an editorial data report: bold condensed display headings, monospace metadata/metrics, white/slate base, and #00aa91 used as the sole signature accent outside platform badge colors.
- KPI summary tiles at the top give immediate quantitative context before the user scrolls.
- Teal accent bar at the very top of the page establishes brand identity immediately.
